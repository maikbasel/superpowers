# 3. A proxy covers a single round only

Date: 2026-10-22
Status: Proposed

## Context

Proxies exist for members who miss a round. A proxy that lasts would give a holder a standing say over another member's votes.

## Decision

An appointment belongs to one round. Nothing carries over to later rounds, and the club keeps no proxy registry.

## Consequences

An absent member names a proxy again for each round they miss. Standing proxies and withdrawing an appointment are out of scope and would need a new decision.

## Rejected options

- **A standing-proxy registry on the club.** Proxies are single-round, and a standing registry would let delegation outlive the absence it was for.
