# 2. A member holds at most one proxy per round

Date: 2026-10-22
Status: Proposed

## Context

At another club a few people collected everyone's vote. Proxy voting must not allow that here.

## Decision

In a round, a member who already holds a proxy is refused a second one.

## Consequences

No holder can cast more than their own vote plus one proxy vote in a round. Members who want to cover several absentees cannot be used as a single point of delegation.

## Rejected options

- **No limit on proxies per holder.** Lets a few people collect everyone's vote.
