# 5. Naming a new proxy replaces the earlier appointment

Date: 2026-10-22
Status: Proposed

## Context

Withdrawing an appointment is out of scope, yet a member may name the wrong person or change their mind before the round closes. This was my call, not yet confirmed with my human partner.

## Decision

Naming a new proxy for the same absent member replaces the earlier appointment, as long as the new holder is free (ADR 2).

## Consequences

A mistaken appointment can be corrected without a withdraw feature. The earlier holder loses the appointment silently.

## Rejected options

- **Refuse a second appointment for the same absent member.** With no withdrawal, a wrong appointment would stand for the whole round.
